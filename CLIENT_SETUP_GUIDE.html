Payment & Shipping
Account Setup Guide

This guide walks you through creating your own Razorpay (payment gateway) and Shiprocket (shipping) accounts. These accounts must be in your name / business name so that money goes directly to your bank.

Razorpay — Payments
Shiprocket — Shipping
~1–2 Hours Total
Dear Client,
To make your website accept online payments and ship orders to customers, two accounts need to be created by you (the business owner). The developer cannot create these on your behalf because:
  • Money from orders goes directly into your bank account — so the account must be yours.
  • KYC (identity verification) requires your business PAN, GST, and bank details.
  • Shiprocket contracts are linked to your business address and pickup location.
Once you complete these steps, share the API keys / credentials (marked in each section) with your developer. That's all you need to do.

What You'll Be Creating

Account 1
Razorpay — to receive payments via UPI, Cards, Net Banking
Account 2
Shiprocket — to ship orders with Blue Dart, DTDC, Delhivery etc.
Your effort
Create accounts, upload documents, share 2 sets of keys
After this
Developer plugs in the keys — website goes live
💳  Part 1 of 2 — Payment Gateway
Razorpay Setup & KYC
Accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets. Settlements go to your bank account in 2 business days.
Phase A — Create Account & Get Test Keys ~15 minutes
  1. 1
    Open Razorpay and sign up
    Click the link below. Register using your business email address. Verify your email when prompted.
    🔗  https://dashboard.razorpay.com/signup
  2. 2
    Go to Settings → API Keys
    After logging in, click Settings in the left sidebar → then click API Keys.
  3. 3
    Generate Test Key
    Make sure you are in Test Mode (toggle is at the top of the dashboard).
    Click "Generate Test Key". You'll see two values:
    Key ID: rzp_test_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
    Key Secret: XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
  4. 4
    Copy and send these two keys to your developer
    ⚠️ The Key Secret is shown only once. Copy it immediately and save it in a secure note (e.g., Google Keep, Notes app, or a password manager). Then share both values with your developer.
ℹ️
These are Test Keys — they let the developer test payments without real money. You'll generate Live Keys after KYC is approved (Phase B below).
Phase B — Complete KYC to Accept Real Payments ~30 minutes + 1–3 days approval
⚠️
KYC is required by RBI regulations before Razorpay allows real payment settlements. Until KYC is approved, payments can be tested but money won't reach your bank.
  1. 1
    Click "Activate Account" in your Razorpay dashboard
    A banner appears at the top of the dashboard. Click "Activate Account" or go to Account & Settings → Activation.
  2. 2
    Fill in your Business Details
    Select your Business Type — choose the one that matches:
    If you are…Select this
    Running the business in your own nameProprietorship
    Registered as Pvt Ltd companyPrivate Limited Company
    Partnership firmPartnership
    Business Category: Select "Retail" → Sub-category: "Home Improvement / Bathroom Fittings" (or closest match).
  3. 3
    Upload the required documents
    Have these documents ready as scanned files (PDF, JPG, or PNG):
    Document What it is Required?
    Business PAN Card PAN card of the company or proprietor Must
    Cancelled Cheque Cheque leaf with your bank account number (or bank passbook front page) Must
    GST Certificate GST registration certificate Must (if GST registered)
    Owner Aadhar Card Aadhar card of the proprietor / director Must
    Owner PAN Card PAN card of the proprietor / director (personal) Must
    Address Proof Electricity bill or rental agreement (last 3 months) Must
    Certificate of Incorporation MCA certificate — only if Pvt Ltd / LLP Only for Pvt Ltd
  4. 4
    Add your Bank Account for settlements
    Enter your business bank account number and IFSC code. Razorpay will do a ₹1 penny deposit to verify it — confirm that penny drop in the dashboard to activate the account.
  5. 5
    Wait for Razorpay approval (1–3 business days)
    You'll get an email when your account is activated. During this time, your website can still be tested with test keys — it just won't accept real money yet.
  6. 6
    After approval — Generate Live Keys and share with developer
    Go to Settings → API Keys → Switch the toggle from Test to Live → Click "Generate Live Key".

    You'll see a new pair:
    Live Key ID: rzp_live_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
    Live Key Secret: XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
    ⚠️ Copy the Live Key Secret immediately — it's shown only once. Share both values with your developer.
✅
Once live keys are shared, the developer will update the website. From that point, real customer payments will go directly to your bank account on a T+2 settlement cycle (2 business days).
Razorpay Fees — What They Charge
Transaction TypeFee
Domestic Cards, UPI, Net Banking, Wallets2% per transaction
EMI transactions3% per transaction
International Cards3% per transaction
GST on fees18% on the 2% fee

Example: Customer pays ₹1,000 → Razorpay deducts ₹20 (2%) + ₹3.60 (GST on fee) = ₹976.40 settled to your bank.

📦  Part 2 of 2 — Shipping
Shiprocket Setup
Ship orders across India with multiple courier partners (Blue Dart, DTDC, Delhivery, Ecom Express, and more) from a single dashboard.
Step-by-Step: Create Your Shiprocket Account ~20 minutes
  1. 1
    Register on Shiprocket
    Use your business email address to register.
    🔗  https://app.shiprocket.in/register
  2. 2
    Choose a plan — Start with Free
    Shiprocket offers a free plan to get started. You can upgrade later as order volume grows. Select "Lite" or "Free" plan when prompted.
  3. 3
    Fill in your Company Profile
    Go to Settings → Company Details and fill in:
    FieldWhat to Enter
    Company NameYour registered business name (e.g., OZOBATH)
    Business TypeE-Commerce / Retail
    GSTINYour GST number (if registered)
    Business AddressYour office / warehouse address
    Contact NumberYour business phone number
  4. 4
    Add a Pickup Address
    This is the address where couriers will come to collect your parcels. Go to Settings → Pickup Addresses → Add New Address.
    📌
    Write down the Pincode of your pickup address — share this with your developer. This is required to configure the shipping system.
  5. 5
    Add Bank Account for COD Remittances
    If you plan to offer Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, go to Settings → Bank Details and add your business bank account. Shiprocket will remit COD collections to this account on a weekly basis.
  6. 6
    Share your login credentials with your developer
    The shipping integration uses your Shiprocket email and password to connect automatically — no separate API key is needed. Share these two values:
    Shiprocket Email: your-registered@email.com
    Shiprocket Password: your-password
    Pickup Pincode: 560001 (replace with yours)
    🔐
    Share these credentials only with your trusted developer via a secure channel (WhatsApp, direct message — not over email). Change your password after the developer has configured the system if you prefer.
How Shipping Works After Setup
StepWhat HappensWho Does It
1. Order Placed Customer places order on your website Customer
2. Order Confirmed Admin confirms payment in the admin panel You (or your staff)
3. Create Shipment One click in the admin panel sends order to Shiprocket You / Staff
4. Choose Courier Admin picks the best courier (by price or speed) You / Staff
5. Print Label Shipping label is auto-generated — print and stick on parcel You / Staff
6. Courier Pickup Courier arrives at your pickup address and collects parcel Courier Partner
7. Auto Tracking Customer gets real-time tracking updates on the website Automatic

Final Checklist — What to Share with Developer
Once you complete both setups, share these with your developer and you're done.
  • Razorpay Test Key ID — starts with rzp_test_
  • Razorpay Test Key Secret — shown once during key generation
  • Razorpay KYC submitted — confirmation email received from Razorpay
  • Razorpay Live Key ID — starts with rzp_live_ (share after KYC approval)
  • Razorpay Live Key Secret — share after KYC approval
  • Shiprocket registered email — the email used to sign up
  • Shiprocket password — the account password
  • Pickup Pincode — the pincode of your pickup/warehouse address
  • Pickup address added in Shiprocket Settings
  • Bank account added in both Razorpay and Shiprocket for settlements
🎉
That's it! Once you share these details with your developer, they will configure the website to accept payments and create shipments. Your website will be ready to go live.
