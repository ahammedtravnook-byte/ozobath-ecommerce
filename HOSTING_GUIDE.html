👋 Hello!

Your OZOBATH website is built and ready. We just need two things from you to make it live on the internet — purchase a domain and give our team access to your hosting account. That's it! We'll handle all the technical work.

1
Purchase Your Domain & Hosting
On Hostinger — takes about 5 minutes
1
Open Hostinger Website

Go to www.hostinger.in and click Sign Up to create a free account (or log in if you have one).

2
Search for Your Domain

In the domain search bar, type ozobath.com and press Enter.

🔍 If ozobath.com is not available, try ozobath.in — it works equally well.
3
Choose a Hosting Plan

Select the Business Web Hosting plan — it includes a free domain, free SSL (https://), and everything we need to run the website.

✅
Recommended: Business Plan (1 or 2 Year)

This plan supports Node.js (our backend technology) and comes with a free domain included — so you may not even need to pay separately for the domain!

4
Complete the Purchase

Add to cart, choose your plan duration, and pay via UPI, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Card. You'll receive a confirmation email from Hostinger.

💡
Tip

The 2-year plan is usually cheaper per month and comes with better deals. Check if there's a discount coupon on the Hostinger homepage.

2
Give Our Team Access
Add us as a collaborator so we can deploy your website
1
Log into Hostinger hPanel

After purchase, go to hpanel.hostinger.com and log in to your account.

2
Go to Account Settings

Click on your Profile icon (top-right corner) → then click Account from the dropdown menu.

3
Find "Manage Access" or "Team Members"

Look for a section called Team Members or Manage Access in the account settings page. Click on it.

📍 It may also be under: Settings → Security → Manage Access
4
Invite Our Developer

Click Add Team Member or Invite Collaborator, then enter our email address:

ahammedmass24@gmail.com

Set the role to Administrator or Full Access so our team can deploy the website files.

5
Send the Invite

Click Send Invitation. Our team will receive an email and accept it. After that, we'll start deploying your website immediately!

🔐
Your account is safe

Adding a collaborator does NOT share your password. Hostinger's team access system is secure — you can remove access anytime from the same settings page.

3
Share Your Payment & Shipping Details
So we can activate online payments and order delivery tracking

💳 Part A — Razorpay (Online Payments)

1
Login to Razorpay Dashboard

Go to dashboard.razorpay.com and log in with your Razorpay account.

2
Complete KYC (if not done)

Go to Account & Settings → KYC and complete your business verification. This is required to accept real payments. Takes 1–2 working days to approve.

3
Switch to Live Mode

At the top of the dashboard, toggle from Test Mode to Live Mode.

4
Generate Live API Keys

Go to Settings → API Keys → Generate Key. You'll get two values — share both with us:

Key ID Starts with rzp_live_
Key Secret Long random string shown once — copy it immediately
⚠️
Important

The Key Secret is shown only once. Copy and save it immediately before closing that page.

🚚 Part B — Shiprocket (Order Shipping & Tracking)

1
Create a Shiprocket Account

If you don't have one, go to shiprocket.in and sign up with your business details.

2
Share Your Shiprocket Login

Send us the email and password you used to register on Shiprocket. We'll link it to the website so orders are automatically assigned for shipping.

3
Share Your Pickup Pincode

Tell us the pincode of your warehouse / pickup address — this is where Shiprocket will collect orders from for delivery.

📨
How to share these details

Once you have everything ready, WhatsApp or email all the details to us at ahammedmass24@gmail.com and we'll activate payments and shipping for your store immediately.
